# Avian Data Monitoring Portal - Project Information

## Overview

Beginning in 2010, **Colibri Ecological Consulting (Colibri)** has implemented high-resolution aerial photographic surveys of individual nesting bird colonies throughout the northern Gulf shoreline. These efforts support Trustees' capacity to restore, conserve, and manage valuable natural resources and critical habitats.

## Scale of Data Collection

- **Over 350,000 images generated** (2010-2023)
- Manual evaluation and "dotting" process for each image
- Identifies and quantifies individual bird species and nests

## Geographic Coverage

**2010-2023 Survey Coverage:**
- 2010: Corpus Christi, TX to Dry Tortugas, FL (22,312 total - 3,115 dotted)
- 2011: Vermilion Bay, LA to Apalachicola Bay, FL (30,187 total - 2,228 dotted)
- 2012: Vermilion Bay, LA to Apalachicola Bay, FL (22,065 total - 1,630 dotted)
- 2013: Vermilion Bay, LA to Apalachicola Bay, FL (21,049 total - 2,056 dotted)
- 2015: Vermilion Bay, LA to Apalachicola Bay, FL (26,246 total - 3,427 dotted)
- 2018: LA only (26,896 total - 1,796 dotted)
- 2021: Brownsville, TX to Pepperfish Keys, FL (104,325 total - 3,958 dotted)
- 2022: Breton Island, LA and New Harbor Island, LA only (87,072 total - 106 dotted)
- 2023: Breton Island, LA only (80,441 total - 184 dotted)

**Total: ~400,000 aerial images**

## Key Information Derived

- Distribution trends
- Relative abundance
- Nest abundance
- Breeding status

## The Portal

The Water Institute compiled photographs and dotting information within the **Avian Data Monitoring Portal**:
- Geospatially enabled and searchable
- Intended for diverse resource stakeholders
- Emphasis on general public access
- Dashboard feature for custom geographic displays

**Key Features:**
- Total nests and birds by species
- Filtering by year, geographic region, watershed, nesting colony
- Interactive visualizations
