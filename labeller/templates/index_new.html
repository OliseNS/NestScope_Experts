New to Nestperts?

  • Labeling (Easy for anyone): Just draw boxes around birds! AI helps with detection. No bird knowledge needed.
  • Classification (Experts only): Identify species from 72 options. Requires ornithology knowledge. Clustering helps by filtering to 5-10 relevant species.
  • Why two separate jobs? Labeling (where are birds?) is much easier than classification (what species?). This lets non-experts help with labeling!
